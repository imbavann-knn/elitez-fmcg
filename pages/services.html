The Client Experience

What to Expect When You Work With Us

From first brief to final report — here is exactly what happens, and when. Every milestone is documented in our operations SOP.

3 Weeks Before Activation

Brief, Build & Align

We prepare a full Activation Brief documenting all objectives, manpower requirements, logistics, and reporting formats. A shared WhatsApp group is created between your team and our project team — the Client, BD Manager, Ops Manager, and Ops Executive all in one channel from Day 1. Recruitment starts immediately, targeting 80% roster fill by the Monday of your activation week.

Activation Brief documented and signed off WhatsApp project group live Recruitment pipeline open
1 Week Before Activation

Trained, Tested & Cleared

Structured training sessions run at our Kallang office — maximum 25 pax per session, covering company SOP, product knowledge, sales pitch, FAQ, role play exercises, and BLINK app setup. Every promoter must pass a product assessment before they are cleared for deployment. No exceptions. Uniforms, premiums, and logistics are distributed and acknowledged with signatures.

100% must pass product quiz to deploy Max 25 pax per training session All logistics distributed and signed for
3 Days Before Activation

BLINK Live, Standby Locked

All promoter profiles are created in BLINK with deployment schedules confirmed and accepted by each promoter. A 5% standby roster is confirmed — trained, briefed, and on-call for the weekend. The full deployment sheet is finalised with outlet assignments, timing, transport allowances, and contact details, and shared with your team for final sign-off.

BLINK deployment schedules live 5% standby confirmed and briefed Full deployment sheet sent to your team
Activation Day

Live, Verified & Reporting Within the Hour

Promoters check in 10 minutes before deployment via BLINK's geolocation system — verified to their outlet location. You receive an attendance report within 30 minutes of deployment time. On Day 1, setup photos showing promoter presentation, booth setup, and shelf placement are sent to you within 2 hours. If a promoter is a no-show, a standby is deployed within 2 hours and you are notified immediately.

Attendance report within 30 minutes of start time Setup photos within 2 hours on Day 1 Standby on-site within 2 hours if needed
Throughout the Campaign

Weekly Mystery Visits & Same-Day Intel

Our ops team conducts unannounced outlet visits and mystery shopping every week — assessing promoter engagement, product knowledge, booth presentation, and crowd flow. Any competitor promotions or new in-store activities spotted are photographed and reported to you the same day. Daily sales are logged into BLINK by promoters within 1 hour of shift end.

Weekly mystery shopping at all outlets Competitor promos photographed and reported same day Daily sales logged via BLINK within 1 hour of close
Post-Campaign

Full Report in 48 Hours. Debrief Included.

Within 2 days of your activation, you receive a complete campaign report: sales figures, attendance records, curated photo evidence, GWP redemption data, and on-ground feedback. Inventory is tallied and all logistics returned within 1 week. Every campaign closes with a formal debrief — what worked, what to optimise, and specific recommendations for your next activation. We end with insights, not just invoices.

Full sales + attendance report within 2 days All photos archived and sent to you Inventory tallied within 1 week Debrief and next-campaign recommendations included
