Why Elitez FMCG

Not Just Promises — Documented Guarantees

Any agency can say they're reliable. We can show you the SOP behind every claim.

01

Attendance Confirmed in 30 Minutes

Every promoter checks in via BLINK — our proprietary geolocation-verified app. You receive an attendance report within 30 minutes of deployment time. Not end of day. Not when you chase us. Thirty minutes.

02

5% Standby, Every Single Weekend

A trained, briefed standby roster is confirmed every Wednesday before your weekend activation. If a promoter calls in sick on Saturday morning, a replacement is on-site within 2 hours. Your campaign never goes dark.

03

60% Campaign Veterans, Minimum

At least 60% of every deployment comes from our regular roster — promoters with 3+ months of Elitez history. We assign first to brand veterans, then by product category experience. You get proven performers, not new faces.

04

Nobody Deploys Without Passing the Quiz

Training covers product knowledge, selling lines, objection handling, and BLINK setup. Every promoter must pass a product assessment before they're cleared for activation. 100% pass rate required — no exceptions, no shortcuts.

05

Sales Report in Your Inbox in 48 Hours

Promoters log sales into BLINK within 1 hour of shift end. You receive a full sales report — data, photos, and on-ground feedback — within 2 days of every activation weekend. No waiting until the next billing cycle.

06

Competitor Intel, Same Day, Included

Our ops team and promoters walk the home bays. Competitor promotions, new SKUs, and shelf changes are photographed and reported to you the same day. You know what's happening beside your product before your competitors know you know.
