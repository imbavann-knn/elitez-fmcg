How We Work

From Brief to Breakdown

A proven 5-step process refined over 15 years of FMCG activations in Singapore.

01

Brief & Scope

You tell us your brand, your objective, your timeline, and your budget. We ask the right questions upfront to make sure there are no surprises downstream. Most proposals come back within 48 hours.

02

Strategy & Planning

Our team develops the activation strategy, selects venues, builds the promoter roster, sources equipment, and creates the operational runbook. You review and sign off.

03

Briefing & Training

Every promoter gets a product briefing specific to your brand. We cover product knowledge, selling points, objection handling, and brand presentation standards before anyone goes on the floor.

04

Execution & Oversight

We run the show. Our ops team is on-site or on-call for every deployment. We handle attendance, logistics, issues, and escalations in real time so you don't have to.

05

Reporting & Debrief

Post-campaign, you receive a full report: attendance records, sales data, consumer engagement numbers, photo evidence, and recommendations for the next activation. We close every campaign with insights, not just invoices.
