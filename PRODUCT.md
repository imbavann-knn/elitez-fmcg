# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Brand managers, marketing leads and procurement teams at FMCG companies operating in Singapore (Reckitt, P&G, Nestlé, Carlsberg, Unilever and similar). They are evaluating field marketing partners — comparing agencies on credibility, scale and capability before sending a brief or requesting a quote. They visit on desktop during work hours, in a commercial decision-making mindset.

Secondary: beauty brand managers and retail buyers assessing Elitez's beauty promoter specialisation.

## Product Purpose

The Elitez FMCG website exists to convert brand-side visitors into enquiries and proposals. It showcases four service pillars (Brand Activations, Promoter Deployment, Supermarket Merchandising, Digital Campaigns), the BLINK field management platform, beauty promoter specialisation, and a rental inventory of activation equipment. Success means a visitor leaves confident enough to submit the contact form or request a demo.

## Brand Personality

Bold, Agile, Results-Driven. The brand is a high-energy field marketing operator — not a consulting firm, not a staffing agency directory. It moves fast, deploys at scale, and proves performance with data. Tone is direct, confident and action-oriented. Never passive or corporate-bland.

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate HR/staffing agency sites: boring blue-grey palettes, handshake stock photos, no visual identity
- Flashy startup landing pages: purple gradients, excessive particle animations, techy buzzwords disconnected from ground-level execution
- Traditional events company sites: event photography collages, formal serif fonts, ceremonial tone
- Cheap promoter agency directories: cluttered layouts, low-quality imagery, no visual hierarchy, price-list mentality

## Design Principles

1. **Ground every claim** — statistics, client logos and real activation photography anchor credibility. Never let the design feel like a brochure without proof.
2. **Energy through restraint** — the orange/black palette is aggressive enough; typography and whitespace should control the pace, not amplify noise.
3. **Operators, not consultants** — the voice and layout should feel like a team that has deployed 300 promoters this week, not one writing decks about it.
4. **Conversion over decoration** — every section should have a clear next action. Design serves the funnel.
5. **Consistent system, not ad-hoc pages** — every page (Services, BLINK, Beauty, Inventory, About, Contact) shares the same nav, footer, typographic scale and component vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. High contrast text on orange backgrounds is a known risk area — white text on #FF5500 must be checked. No specific known user needs beyond standard web accessibility.
