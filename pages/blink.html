What is BLINK?

Platform Architecture

THREE PORTALS.
ONE ECOSYSTEM.

Each stakeholder has their own purpose-built interface. All three are connected to the same live data — so everyone is always on the same page.

Admin Portal
Elitez Operations Team

The engine room. Elitez's internal team manages campaign setup, promoter allocation, outlet scheduling, and system oversight from here.

  • Campaign setup: brands, SKUs, training decks, shift rules
  • Smart promoter matching + conflict-free scheduling
  • Dual-stream payroll automation (CPF & non-CPF, Excel export)
  • Approval engine: rate overrides, blacklist, budget alerts
  • Field visit quality scoring with photo evidence
  • Full audit log — every action, actor, timestamp
Client Portal
Brand Managers & Procurement

Your window into the campaign. Real-time visibility, quality checks, live sales data, and automated reporting — without needing to call or email anyone.

  • Live check-in photos by outlet, timestamped
  • Real-time sales by SKU, conversion % & premium redemptions
  • Pre-deployment promoter portfolio PDF for approval
  • Post-Project Evaluation (PPE) with photos & ops assessment
  • Training completion status per promoter
  • Multi-user login — brand team + procurement
Promoter Portal
On-Ground Promoters

A simple, mobile-first app for promoters on the ground. Check in, log sales, complete tasks, and stay connected to the campaign — from any smartphone.

  • Shift acceptance with store map + pay breakdown
  • Training deck + AI-generated quiz (100% pass required)
  • Camera-only check-in — fraud-proof, no gallery upload
  • Sales entry by SKU with voice-to-text (Gemini-powered)
  • Premium & break tracking mid-shift
  • Payslips, claims & uniform return history
Under The Hood

SIX THINGS BLINK AUTOMATES FOR YOU

01 · Matching
SMART PROMOTER MATCHING

Distance-to-outlet, brand experience, conflict history, recency scoring — matched in seconds, not hours of manual filtering.

02 · Training
AI QUIZ GENERATION

Upload your training deck. Gemini generates the quiz. 100% pass rate required before any promoter is cleared for their first shift.

03 · Premium
PREMIUM REDEMPTION TRACKING

Receipt number, spend amount, photo — captured per transaction. No reconciliation surprises at campaign end. Live count visible to client.

04 · Payroll
DUAL-STREAM PAYROLL

Non-CPF weekly + CPF biweekly, processed separately. Rounded shift hours, break payability, transport & PH allowances — Excel output to HR Finance.

05 · WhatsApp
WHATSAPP INTEGRATION

Job-confirm messages, shift reminders, and one-click chat with promoters on the ground — built in. No third-party tools. Manager-editable templates, no dev needed.

06 · Compliance
COMPLIANCE & AUDIT TRAIL

Every action logged with actor, timestamp & before/after state. NRIC auto-purged at 12 months. PDPA acknowledgements captured. Bank details require manager approval.
