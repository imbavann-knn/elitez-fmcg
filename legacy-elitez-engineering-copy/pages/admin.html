Competitive Positioning — FMCG Specialisation vs Scale

Elitez FMCG — SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • 800+ WSQ-certified promoter roster — largest specialist FMCG database in SG
  • 4,000sqft warehouse with deployable equipment reduces client cost
  • 15+ years track record with marquee FMCG clients
  • Deploy 300 pax in a single weekend — unmatched operational scale
  • Ops team with 30 combined years in SG FMCG
  • One-stop-shop: strategy, staffing, logistics, reporting

Weaknesses

  • Limited proprietary digital marketing capability vs pure-digital agencies
  • Primarily Singapore-market focused — limited SEA footprint
  • Smaller brand recognition vs larger regional competitors
  • Less corporate events capability (intentional but limits TAM)

Opportunities

  • Post-COVID FMCG activation surge — brands reinvesting in BTL
  • Growing demand for integrated ATL+BTL campaigns
  • SEA expansion — Malaysia, Indonesia FMCG markets growing
  • Digital+physical hybrid campaigns becoming standard
  • New FMCG entrants needing end-to-end activation partner

Threats

  • General staffing agencies (e.g. Stafflink) expanding into FMCG promoter space
  • Gig economy platforms commoditising promoter deployment
  • Client budget cuts shifting spend to digital-only campaigns
  • Rising promoter wage expectations (SSG/MOM minimum wage trends)

Win / Loss Factors in RFPs

Why We Win

Roster depth gives clients confidence we can scale. Warehouse assets reduce their cost. 15yr track record is a de-risk. Ops team's SG-specific knowledge prevents typical activation pitfalls.

Why We Lose

Rate card perceived as higher than generalist staffing agencies. Clients sometimes choose cheaper option without understanding quality gap. Limited digital marketing portfolio vs integrated agencies.

Counter-Strategy

Lead with post-activation ROI data. Show cost-per-consumer-engagement vs competitor. Offer pilot campaign pricing for new clients. Build digital capability to compete on integrated briefs.
