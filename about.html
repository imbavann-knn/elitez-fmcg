How We Started

Born on the Retail Floor

Elitez FMCG was founded in 2009 with a simple belief: that great brand activations aren't about big budgets — they're about the right people, in the right place, executing with precision.

We started small, staffing weekend promotions for a handful of FMCG clients in Singapore's heartland supermarkets. Word spread fast. Brands noticed that our promoters sold more, stayed longer, and represented the product better than anyone else.

Today, Elitez FMCG is one of Singapore's most trusted BTL agencies, deploying hundreds of promoters every weekend across the island. Our 4,000sqft warehouse holds the equipment. Our roster holds the talent. Our ops team holds it all together.
